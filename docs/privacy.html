AHHHHHH Today

Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026 · Versión en español

AHHHHHH Today is a private app used by a single family to help a student organise her day. This page explains which data it uses, what for, and what it does not do with it.

Google data it accesses

Only with the explicit permission of the person who connects their account on Google's consent screen, and always read-only unless stated otherwise:

  • Google Classroom: classes, assignments with their due dates, and announcements, to show them in the daily plan.
  • Gmail: messages, to detect relevant notices from the school and activities (timetable changes, deadlines, permission slips…). Anything detected is shown to the family for review and approval; nothing is applied automatically.
  • Google Calendar: the app only creates and manages its own calendar ("AHHHHHH"), where it writes activities and tasks if export is turned on. It does not read or change any other calendar.

Other data

  • What the family enters: activities, events, tasks, addresses (e.g. home) and preferences.
  • The device location, only with permission and only to estimate travel time to the next activity.

Who it is shared with

No one except the services needed for the app to work, and only what is strictly necessary:

  • Anthropic (Claude): the text of new emails and posts is sent to extract dates, deadlines and notices. It is not used to train models.
  • Google Maps Platform: the origin and destination of each trip, to estimate travel time with traffic.
  • Google Firebase: hosting of the app and storage of its data.

Data is never sold, never used for advertising and never passed on to third parties.

Limited use of Google data

AHHHHHH Today's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept while the family uses the app. Disconnecting the account in the app deletes the stored permissions.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time from the Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To request deletion of all data, write to the support email shown on Google's consent screen.

Minors

The app is used by a minor with the knowledge and supervision of her family, who set it up and connect the accounts.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to the support email shown on Google's consent screen when connecting the account.
